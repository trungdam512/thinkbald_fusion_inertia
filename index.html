Investor Verdict · CFS
The Safe Bet
Most mature build, strongest corporate partnerships, and the clearest path to grid power in the early 2030s. But valuations are rich and the upside for new investors is limited. Best for strategics and late-stage funds seeking portfolio insurance.
Investor Verdict · Helion
The Moonshot Clock
The 2028 Microsoft deadline is either the most credible commercial fusion contract ever written, or a pressure cooker that forces a valuation reset. Highest binary risk. Unique direct-electricity model could be transformative if it works.
Investor Verdict · Inertia
The Smart New Money
Early entry, NIF-proven physics, and a founding team that combines world-class science with proven tech-company execution. The manufacturing scale challenge is real, but the VC math is the most compelling of the three. Bessemer and GV are not wrong to be here.
